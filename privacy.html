Privacy Policy

Last updated August 2026

The short version

Kloom has no accounts, no servers and no analytics. Everything you save stays on your device, and — if you turn Cloud Sync on — in your own private iCloud. We cannot see it, and we never sell or share it.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no sign-up, no profile and no login, so we never receive your name, email address or any identifier. We run no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking SDKs.

What the app stores, and where

Your links, titles, notes, attached images, folders, spaces and saved offline articles are written to a private container on your device, shared only between Kloom and its Share Extension.

With Cloud Sync on, that data also syncs through Apple’s CloudKit into your own private iCloud database. It is tied to your Apple Account, and Apple’s terms govern it. The developer has no access to it.

When the app uses the network

Saving a link fetches that page’s title, description, icon and preview image directly from the website you saved. Saving an article for offline reading downloads its text and images the same way.

Those requests go straight from your device to that website — there is no Kloom server in between. The site sees an ordinary visit, including your IP address, just as your browser would. We receive nothing.

Purchases

Pro subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple. Payment happens in the App Store; we never see your card, billing address or Apple Account details. The app only asks Apple whether an active subscription exists, and remembers that yes/no answer on your device.

Deleting your data

Deleting a link moves it to Trash, and Trash is purged automatically after the retention window or immediately when you delete it there. Removing the app deletes everything stored on the device.

If Cloud Sync was on, the iCloud copy is removed from Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Kloom. Since we hold no copy, there is nothing to request from us.

Children

Kloom is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect anything from them — or, in fact, from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version appears here with a new date. Material changes will also be noted in the App Store release notes.
