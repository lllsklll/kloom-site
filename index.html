Save links from anywhere.

Share a link from any app — Safari, YouTube, X, WhatsApp — and Kloom keeps it as a clean preview card. Organised into spaces, readable offline, and stored only on your device.

Coming to the App Store

What it does

One tap to save

The iOS Share Sheet does the work. Add a note and pick a space before it saves, or just tap and move on.

Spaces and folders

Keep work, reading and personal links apart, then group the related ones into folders inside a space.

Read offline

Archive a clean, readable copy of an article. It opens instantly later — no network, no clutter, no cookie banners.

Yours alone

No account, no sign-up, no analytics. Everything lives on your device and, if you want, your own private iCloud.

Privacy

There is nothing to collect.

Kloom has no servers. Your links never pass through us, because there is no “us” in the middle — the app talks to the website you saved and to your own iCloud, and to nothing else.
